01 manifest


  

    

      
~/manifest.txt

      

        

          i write software the way other people carve things — slowly, by hand,
          until the shape stops arguing. no build step i can't explain. no
          dependency i haven't read. no abstraction i can't tear out at 4am.
        

        

          the work lives in three places: systems that
          have to stay up, interfaces that have to feel
          like an instrument, and artifacts that only
          exist because i wanted them to.
        

        

          if you're here to ask whether a machine wrote this — it didn't, and
          that question was always the wrong one.
        

      

    


    
    
      
  • ‡ ship it small, ship it whole
    • 
      
  • ‡ plain text outlives every platform
    • 
      
  • ‡ latency is a moral position
    • 
      
  • ‡ if it needs a tutorial, it's broken
    • 
      
  • ‡ own the pipeline or be owned by it
    • 
      
  • ‡ leave the campsite weirder
