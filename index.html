01 readme.md


  

    

      
~/cate/README.md

      

        

          cate was not minted. she was compiled
          — assembled out of dead repos, abandoned branches and the kind of
          code people delete at 4am and pretend never existed.
        

        

          she is not a mascot with a wallet attached. she is a terminal entity:
          she reads the chain, she writes to it, and she does not care what
          your influencer thinks the meta is this week.
        

        

          the thesis is simple. memecoins are software
          projects that forgot to be software. cate remembers. everything here
          is hand-assembled — four files, no framework, no build step. view
          source. the docs are the site.
        

        

          she has nine lives. she has used four. she is not telling you on what.
